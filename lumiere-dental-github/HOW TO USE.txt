LUMIERE DENTAL - HOW TO USE
===========================

1) START THE WEBSITE
   Double-click:  Start Website.bat
   A black window opens (keep it open) and the website opens in your browser
   at  http://localhost:4173

2) BOOK AN APPOINTMENT
   Click "Book a visit" (or "Book your visit"), pick a treatment, dentist, day and time,
   enter name, email and phone, and click "Confirm booking".

3) SEE IT IN THE SPREADSHEET
   Open  Bookings.csv  in this folder (it opens in Excel).
   Each booking is one row: date received, reference, status, treatment, dentist,
   date, time, patient name, email, phone and note.
   The black window also prints every new booking as it arrives.

   - Change the Status column to Confirmed / Completed / Cancelled as you like.
     "Cancelled" makes that time bookable again on the website.
   - If Excel has the file open while someone books, the booking is kept safely in
     Bookings-pending.csv and moved into Bookings.csv as soon as you close Excel.
     (Close and reopen Bookings.csv to see the newest rows.)

4) GOOGLE SHEET COPY (already connected)
   Every booking is also copied to your Google Sheet "Lumiere Dental - Bookings"
   in your Google Drive. The "Google Sheet" column in Bookings.csv shows whether
   the copy worked.

5) STOP THE WEBSITE
   Close the black window.

6) PUT IT ONLINE FOR EVERYONE (Vercel)
   Double-click:  Deploy to Vercel.bat
   - The first time, your browser opens: log in to Vercel (Google, GitHub or email).
   - It uploads the site and opens your live address (like https://lumiere-dental.vercel.app).
   - The address is saved in vercel-url.txt. Share it with anyone.
   - On the online site, bookings go to your Google Sheet "Lumiere Dental - Bookings"
     (Bookings.csv is only for the Start Website.bat version on this computer).
   - Changed something? Double-click it again to update the live site.

IMPORTANT
   - Use Start Website.bat. Opening index.html directly, or viewing it inside the
     Claude app preview, cannot write to Bookings.csv (a web page on its own is not
     allowed to write files on your computer).
   - index.html on its own still works for sharing the design, and it sends bookings
     to the Google Sheet when opened in Chrome or Edge.
   - Needs Node.js (already installed on this computer).
